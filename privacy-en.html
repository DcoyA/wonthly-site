Privacy Policy

Effective October 21, 2026

In short
  • If you use Wonthly without signing in, no personal data is sent to any server. All records are stored encrypted on your device.
  • If you sign in and turn on sync, records are encrypted on your device before upload. The server, including us, can't read them.
  • No ads, no advertising identifiers, no analytics. We never sell or share your data.
  • Data read from Apple Health stays on your device and is never sent to a server.

1. What we process

Without signing in

Wonthly collects nothing and sends nothing to a server. Everything you log — periods, symptoms, mood, notes, medications, temperature, appointments — is stored in an encrypted database on your device, with its key kept in the device's secure storage (iOS Keychain).

With sign-in and sync (optional)

ItemDetails
AccountAccount identifier, sign-in method (Kakao, Apple, Google), and the email address your sign-in provider shares, if any
Encrypted recordsRecords encrypted on your device and their edit times. Contents and dates are encrypted and unknown to the server.
Encrypted data keyA key encrypted with a recovery key that only you have. We don't know your recovery key and can't decrypt it.

Apple Health data

Only the items you turn on in settings (sleep, resting heart rate, temperature, steps) are read and used on your device for cycle insights. This data is never sent to a server, synced, or used for advertising or marketing. Periods are written to Apple Health only if you turn that on.

What we don't collect

Advertising identifiers, location, contacts, photos, or usage analytics.

2. Why

Only to sign you in and to provide end-to-end encrypted sync so you can see your records on multiple devices.

3. Retention and deletion

Account data and encrypted records are kept until you delete your account. You can do this anytime in Me › Account and sync › Delete account, which immediately deletes your account and records from the server. Copies in server backups are removed within 7 days. Records on your device are removed when you delete the app.

4. Sharing

We don't sell or share personal data with third parties.

6. Security

  • Full encryption of the on-device database
  • End-to-end encryption of synced records (AES-256-GCM), with keys handled only by your devices and your recovery key
  • Row-level security so each signed-in user can only access their own data
  • Encrypted connections (TLS)
  • Optional app lock with Face ID or device passcode

7. Your rights

You can edit or delete records and delete your account in the app at any time. To request access to, correction of, or deletion of data on our server, contact us below.

8. Children

Sign-in and sync are for users 14 and older. Younger users can use Wonthly without signing in, in which case no personal data leaves the device.

9. Health information

Predictions and insights are for reference only and must not be used for diagnosis or contraception. Wonthly is not a medical device.
